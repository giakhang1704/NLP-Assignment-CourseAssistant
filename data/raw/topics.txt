chapter_id: CH07

CHAPTER 9: AMBIGUITY RESOLUTION: SELECTIONAL RESTRICTIONS
chapter_id: CH09
part: Part II: Semantic Interpretation
keywords: selectional restrictions, abstraction hierarchy, constraint satisfaction, semantic networks, word sense disambiguation
summary:
This chapter investigates techniques for filtering sense ambiguities using semantic constraints known as selectional restrictions. By organizing concepts into an abstraction hierarchy of subclasses and semantic networks, the parser enforces restrictions on predicate-argument combinations like verb AGENT and THEME requirements. It frames disambiguation as a constraint satisfaction problem and explores statistical word sense disambiguation using context window probabilities.
